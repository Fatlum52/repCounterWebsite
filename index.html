Track your workouts.
Nothing more, nothing less.

RepCounter is a simple workout tracker for iPhone. Log your sessions, sets, reps and weight, reuse your favourite routines and see your progress. Your data stays in your own iCloud.

Workout sessions

Create a training session, add your exercises and log every set with reps and weight.

  • See all sessions with date and total reps per exercise
  • Each exercise shows sets, total reps and training volume at a glance
  • "Last time" shows what you did in your previous session for the same exercise
  • The weight of the last set is carried over when you add a new set

Notes and media

Write down cues for your next training and attach photos or videos to check your form.

Rest timer

Set your rest time and start the timer. It keeps running on the Lock Screen as a Live Activity and plays an alarm when your rest is over.

Library and templates

Save the exercises you use often and build session templates, so starting a workout takes only a tap.

Explore exercises

Search a large exercise database with images, target muscles, equipment and step by step instructions. Add any exercise to your library with one tap.

Exercise data by AscendAPI.com.

Statistics and settings

See your total sessions, exercises and volume, plus how often and how much you trained each exercise. In the More tab you can choose between English and German.

Light and dark mode

RepCounter follows your system appearance and switches automatically between light and dark mode.

Your data, your iCloud

RepCounter has no account, no ads and no tracking.

Stored in your iCloud

Your workouts are saved in your private iCloud database. We have no access to it.

Synced across devices

Use the same Apple Account on your devices and your data stays in sync automatically.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no third party trackers. Read the privacy policy.
